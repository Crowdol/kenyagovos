# kenyagovos
KenyaGov OS is a secure, unified digital platform that exposes government services and public data through APIs, enabling startups and citizens to build innovative solutions.
It is built on four foundational pillars:
Digital ID – Every citizen and entity has a verifiable, self‑sovereign digital identity.
One Login for All Services – Single Sign‑On (SSO) across all government portals and partner applications.
Zero Paper by Year 5 – All government interactions become fully digital and paperless.
Citizens Own Their Data – Individuals control access to their personal data via consent‑based sharing.
Blockchain Audit Trails – Every transaction is immutably recorded on a distributed ledger for transparency and accountability.
